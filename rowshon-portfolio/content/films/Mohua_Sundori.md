synopsis: |
  Inspired by the timeless folk ballads of Bangladesh’s celebrated \*Mymensingh Geetika\*, this romantic drama explores the blurred boundaries between art, identity, and love. When a young woman portrays a dancer in a theatrical production, she becomes deeply immersed in her character’s emotional world. As the story unfolds on stage, fiction begins to intertwine with reality, awakening unexpected feelings for a fellow actor. What begins as a performance transforms into a passionate journey of love, self-discovery, and emotional awakening, bringing the magic of Bengali folklore to life.
